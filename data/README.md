# Data

`light_vehicle_sales.csv` is downloaded on demand by running `python download_data.py`. It contains FRED series `LTOTALNSA`, Light Weight Vehicle Sales, sourced from the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis. The series is monthly and measured in thousands of units, not seasonally adjusted.

The CSV is excluded from Git. Cite the source as: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, Light Weight Vehicle Sales [LTOTALNSA], retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis: <https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/LTOTALNSA>. FRED identifies the series as public domain with citation requested.
